5. Reporting content, and what we do about it

We review every report within 24 hours. Reports are confidential — we never tell the reported person who reported them.

You can report anything in the app. Tap the ··· menu on a photo, poll, expense, accommodation, squad member, or friend and choose Report; open a photo full-screen and use the flag button; or, for a whole squad, open Squad Settings and tap Report This Squad. You can also email us — see Support.

When content breaks these guidelines we may, depending on severity: remove it, warn the account, suspend the account, or terminate it permanently. Repeated or serious violations lead to termination without warning. We may act on our own initiative as well as in response to reports.

If you think we got it wrong, email us and we will take another look.

6. Blocking someone

You can block any friend or squad member from the ··· menu next to their name. Blocking is silent — the other person is not told.

When you block someone:

  • You stop being friends, and any pending friend request between you is withdrawn.
  • Neither of you can send the other a friend request or a squad invite, and neither of you can find the other by email.
  • In squads you both belong to, you stop seeing each other's photos, polls, and live location.
  • Neither of you leaves any squad. Blocking someone does not remove them, or you, from a trip you are both planning. Removing a member from a squad remains the squad owner's decision. Shared expenses stay visible to both of you, because they record money owed between you.

You can undo a block at any time from Account → Blocked Accounts. Unblocking restores visibility only — it does not make you friends again.

Only your current position is stored — never a location history. Do not share another person's location without their knowledge.
