Squadmap is a group trip-planning app. Create a squad with friends and plan a trip together — dates, a route, where you're staying, shared expenses, photos and polls — and optionally share your live location with that squad while you travel.

Reporting content or blocking someone

Every piece of content in Squadmap can be reported, and any friend or squad member can be blocked.

To report something:

  • A photo — open it full screen and tap the flag button in the top right.
  • A poll, expense, or accommodation — tap the ··· menu on it and choose Report.
  • A squad member — open the squad, tap the ··· menu next to their name, choose Report.
  • A friend — open Friends, tap the ··· menu next to their name, choose Report.
  • A whole squad — open Squad Settings and tap Report This Squad.

Pick a reason, add any details, and submit. You can tick Also block in the same step. We review every report within 24 hours, and the person you reported is never told who reported them.

To block someone: use the same ··· menu and choose Block. See Terms of Use §6 for exactly what blocking does — in short, you stop seeing each other's content and neither of you can contact the other, but nobody is removed from a shared squad.

To unblock someone: Account → Blocked Accounts.

What is and isn't allowed is set out in the Community Guidelines.

Frequently asked questions

How do squads work?

A squad is a private group for one trip. Only people who were invited and accepted can see anything in it. There is no public feed, no discovery, and no way for a stranger to find your squad or push content at you.

How do I invite someone?

Add them as a friend first (Friends → +, by email — they have to accept), then open your squad and add them from your friends list. They will see the invite on their home screen and can accept or decline.

How does live location sharing work?

It is off by default and opt-in per squad, from the Route screen. Only your current position is stored — never a history — and only members of that squad can see it. Turn it off at any time. Squadmap works fully with location permission denied.

For sharing to continue while your phone is locked, iOS needs the Always location permission. With While Using the App, sharing pauses when you leave the app.

How do shared expenses work?

Add an expense, choose who it is split between, and Squadmap tracks who owes what. Mark a share as paid when you settle up; the person who paid confirms it.

How do I delete my account?

Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, friends, expenses, saved locations, and photos, and cannot be undone. Squads you created stay with their remaining members, and ownership passes to the member who joined first.

How do I reset my password?

Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. The reset link is single-use and expires — if it has already been used or has timed out, just request another.

Troubleshooting

  • The security check won't load. Sign-in shows an hCaptcha challenge. If it fails to appear you will see an explanation and a Try Again button — this usually means no connectivity or a content blocker.
  • The confirmation email hasn't arrived. Check your spam folder. You must confirm your address before signing in.
  • My location isn't updating for my squad. Check sharing is on for that squad on the Route screen, and that Squadmap has the Always location permission in iOS Settings.
  • A photo won't upload. Photos are resized before upload but very large files can still fail on a weak connection. Try again on Wi-Fi.
